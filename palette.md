# Magenta:

## Elements:
- originality                           <--
- innovation
- artistic flair
- out-of-the-box thinking
- expressive individuality              <--
- attention attractor (red)
- calm / trustworthy (blue)
- nonconformity / uniqueness            <--
- compassion / emotional connection     <--
- community                             <--
- self-expression / lifestyle           <--

- appeals to younger audiences (saturated, expressive colours)
- associated with fashion, beauty, entertainment, youth culture

Stimulates both left- and right-brain cognitive
processes simultaneously, subtly enhancing pattern
recognition and creativity-driven engagement.

## Uses:
- highlighting creativity / art         |   Story stickers, filters, gradient accents
- reward signals / Call-To-Action       |   Subscription offers, premium feature buttons
- branding / visual differentiation     |   Gradients and tones
- notifications / micro-interactions    |   Subtle highlights for non-critical notifications

## Works with:
- Contrasting neutrals (white, black, grey) to balance vibrancy and maintain readability:
- Green / teal enhance attention without overwhelming users


## Avoid:
- over-saturation and visual fatigue:
    - excessive use (especially on large areas) may strain eyes or reduce clarity
    - bright backgrounds behind text can compromise typography readabiliyt and scanning of content
- magenta often seen as more feminine:
    - may narrow the audience perception of the platform's target
    SOLUTION?: balance with blues
- due to blue-dominant apps, magenta as the primary colour may appear visually jarring
